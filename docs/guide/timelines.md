# Timelines

A **timeline** is a named, chronological log. Each one lives in the sidebar and
holds a series of [entries](./entries) in time order. Think of one timeline per
topic: a client, a project, a person, a recurring theme.

## Creating a timeline

Press **New Timeline** at the top of the sidebar, type a name, and it opens ready
for your first entry. Every new timeline begins with a **Timeline Start** marker so
there's always a clear beginning.

## Renaming, tagging, and deleting

Each timeline has a **⋮** menu in the sidebar:

- **Rename** — change the name at any time.
- **Tags** — add or remove [tags](./tags-and-search) to group and filter
  timelines.
- **Delete** — remove the timeline and all its entries.

::: warning
Deleting a timeline removes its entries and their attachments. There's no undo —
keep an [export](./storage-and-backup#backing-up-with-export-import) if you might
want it back.
:::

## Finding the right timeline

- **Scroll the sidebar** — timelines are listed there, and you can filter them by
  tag.
- **Search** — use the search box in the header to jump to any timeline or entry
  by its content. See [Tags & search](./tags-and-search).

## How many timelines should I have?

Whatever keeps each log readable. A few rules of thumb:

- One **active concern** per timeline (a deal, a project, a trip).
- Use **tags** instead of splitting hairs — e.g. tag several client timelines
  with `client` rather than nesting them.
- It's fine to have lots of small timelines; the sidebar filter and search keep
  them manageable.
