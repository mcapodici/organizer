# Use cases

Organizer is deliberately general — anything that benefits from *"a record over
time, with follow-ups"* fits well. Here are proven ways people use it. Each maps
onto the same handful of features: [timelines](/guide/timelines),
[entries](/guide/entries), [due dates](/guide/todos-and-due-dates),
[tags](/guide/tags-and-search), and [attachments](/guide/attachments).

## Client & deal follow-ups

One timeline per client or deal. Log every call, email, and meeting; attach
quotes and contracts; add a due date to every "next contact". Your **Todos** page
becomes a chase list so no client goes cold.

> Tag client timelines `client` + a status (`active` / `won` / `lost`) to filter
> the sidebar at a glance.

## Project tracking

A timeline per project capturing decisions, progress, and blockers, with
due-dated next steps. See the [Track a project](/tutorials/project-log)
tutorial.

## Personal journal

A private, searchable daily log — words plus photos — stored on your own device.
See [Keep a daily journal](/tutorials/daily-journal).

## Health & symptom log

Record symptoms, appointments, and medication changes with accurate dates
(use **custom timestamps** to backfill). Attach test results and photos. A clear
chronology is exactly what's useful to review — or to show a professional.

## Property & maintenance records

One timeline per property or vehicle. Log services, repairs, and inspections;
attach receipts and warranties; set due dates for the next service. Years later
you have a complete, searchable history.

## Research & reading notes

A timeline per topic or paper. Capture findings as you go, attach PDFs and
screenshots, and search across everything when you're writing up.

## Incident & decision log

For a team or a side project: a dated record of incidents and the decisions made
in response — what happened, what you decided, and why. Invaluable when someone
later asks *"why did we do it this way?"*

## Habit & training log

Daily entries tracking workouts, practice, or any habit. Backfill missed days
with custom timestamps; review the timeline to see your streak and progress.
